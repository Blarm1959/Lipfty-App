How to play

Lipfty 14

Objective. Make a winning pattern on the inner 6×6. Pieces are shared rather than owned by either player.

Opening Four. Four shaped pieces, two of each colour, are placed on the four inner corners. The opponent chooses the colour; the player chooses the corner. The fourth forced placement is automatic.

First normal placement. After the Opening Four, the starter places the first ordinary reserve piece. The other player then chooses Accept or Take position.

Corner rule. An Opening Four piece is blocked from every winning pattern while its matching outer corner is locked.

Release rule. When all six ordinary reserve pieces on an outside edge have gone, both special corner pieces at the ends of that edge are released. Their matching Opening Four pieces immediately become active for all winning patterns.

Learning. Place only. Win with four of one colour horizontally, vertically or diagonally.

Standard. Place, Move or Jump. Move one square in any direction. Jump over one adjacent opposite-colour movable piece into the empty square beyond.

Extreme. Standard plus tight and spaced axis-aligned Square wins.

Move consequence. The mover keeps the originally handed piece. The opponent chooses and places another reserve piece; the mover then places the original piece.

Jump consequence. The jumped piece and originally handed piece are both redeployed in the established Lipfty sequence.
