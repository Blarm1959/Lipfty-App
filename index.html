Lipfty 14.0.1

Opening Four: four shaped pieces are placed on the four corners of the inner 6×6. The opponent chooses the colour; the player chooses the corner.

Main play: Place, Move or Jump as in Lipfty 13.

Corner rule: an Opening Four piece cannot count in any winning pattern while its matching outer corner is locked.

Release rule: when all six ordinary reserve pieces on an outside edge have gone, both corners on that edge are released. Their matching inner corners immediately become fully live for all winning patterns.

Extreme: tight and spaced square wins are also allowed, but a blocked Opening Four piece cannot take part until its matching outer corner has been released.
